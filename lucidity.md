# Preserve lucidity

Lucidity is when work glides. You can see your thoughts and keep them, and the software does not get in the way.

Most software now is a loss of lucidification. It is noisy, you spend your time using it, and the work you meant to do sits there waiting. What you make lives on their servers, behind an account, in a format no other tool can use, so if you leave the product you leave your work as well.

Take the fluff out until what is left still does the job, and do not replace the person. You get better by thinking, not by repeating chores, and not by explaining yourself again to a tool that knows nothing about you. Software is supposed to do the chores for you. It should leave the thinking to you, and it should not make the decisions for you.

Help is fine when you can call it and send it away, a draft or a tidy or something boring, then you on your own again. If the only way to get that is to quit, you are stuck.

A boat without a captain doesn't go anywhere. A butler with no household is without purpose.

All your files should be exportable, software should be open, and you should be able to change it, fork it, or take what you made and go, not get held by a format that only works in one place or a price that ties you down.

Tools should allow for innovation not extraction, but the only way this is possible is if we work together to preserve lucidity.
